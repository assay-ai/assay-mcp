# Changelog

Notable changes to `@goassay-ai/mcp`. Earlier releases are described in their release commits.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-02

Channels are now used by default: `deployments.json` marks `production` (`eip155:4663`) and `staging` (`eip155:46630`) with `"channels": true`. The lanes live in the profile's state file, `{dir}/profiles/<profile>.state.json` (`{dir}` is `~/Library/Application Support/assay-mcp` on macOS, `~/.config/assay-mcp` on Linux, `%USERPROFILE%\AppData\Roaming\assay-mcp` on Windows), created on the first channel payment. The payment core is still `@goassay-ai/sdk` 1.1.0.

### Changed

- **Channel vouchers on.** Every forwarded `tools/call` now carries `assay/paymentSchemes: ["channel-v1", "voucher"]` (except with `--no-paid`), and a paid call signs a channel voucher whenever Assay offers a channel on the built-in escrow. There is no switch to turn channels off.
- A call is still paid per call when no channel can be used: Assay offers none, channels are paused for an hour after `CHANNEL_LIMIT`, `CHANNEL_SIGNER_EXPIRING`, `CHANNEL_NOT_OFFERED` or five `CHANNEL_ROTATE` within an hour, the offer does not match the requirement or the built-in entry, no lane is free, the lane record cannot be read or written, or the chain read of a lane is not agreed.
- The local `e2e` and `anvil` entries stay without channels (their fixture escrow has no channel door).

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-02

Off until a release marks a network as channel-capable in `deployments.json` (none is in this release): nothing changes for a user of 1.0.0. The payment core is `@goassay-ai/sdk` 1.1.0, which adds channel vouchers.

### Added

- **Channel vouchers (`channel-v1`, preview).** A network entry in `deployments.json` may say `"channels": true`. On such a network the proxy adds `assay/paymentSchemes: ["channel-v1", "voucher"]` to every forwarded `tools/call` (never with `--no-paid`), and when Assay answers with a channel offer it signs the contract's `ChannelVoucher` (the SDK's types and lane rules) and sends it as `assay/channelVoucher`. No built-in entry is marked yet.
  - The lanes live in the profile's `state.json`, under the profile lock, so every host on the computer shares one record and two hosts never sign conflicting totals. A lane's new total, and the spend record of what it adds, are written before the voucher is signed. A damaged lane record is never reset: paid calls stop until it is fixed, as for any damaged `state.json`.
  - Every guard runs on the per-call requirement first; the offer must match it and name exactly the built-in escrow, or the call is paid per call. The total is never taken from the server.
  - The daily limit counts only what each voucher adds: the price, or nothing when a voucher refused without being stored is signed again.
  - A lane not checked in the last 10 minutes is checked against the escrow (`channelBooks`, every RPC at one pinned block) outside the lock; a record behind the chain is retired and a new lane opened. Without an agreed read the call is paid per call.
  - A channel voucher that gets no answer (the connection drops, the host's resend fails, or a JSON-RPC error or non-object comes back instead of a result) retires the lane but keeps its pair renewable, since the server may have stored it (SDK spec 12.4, as the SDK does). A debt on that pair can then be renewed by the agent.
  - Still at most one voucher per host call. `CHANNEL_ROTATE` retires the lane (five rotations within an hour stop channel offers for an hour); `CHANNEL_LIMIT`, `CHANNEL_SIGNER_EXPIRING` and `CHANNEL_NOT_OFFERED` re-arm it and stop offering channels for an hour.
  - A spend-policy refusal made before Assay stores the channel voucher re-arms the lane (the next call signs the same pair on the same lane, adding nothing to the spend record): `MCP_VELOCITY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` with `reason` `DUPLICATE_REQUEST`, `CALLS_PER_MINUTE`, `CALLS_PER_HOUR` or `SPEND_PER_HOUR`, `MCP_SPEND_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, `MCP_POLICY_AGENT_PAUSED` and `MCP_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` (the SDK's `laneOutcomeOf`, now given the `assay/error` `reason`). Reason `UNAVAILABLE` (may come after the store), another or no reason still retires the lane.
  - A re-armed pair is re-signed for free only while its first signature could still settle and is under 24 h old; otherwise the lane is retired and the new lane's price is recorded. At most 16 retired lanes are kept per pool. The agent reads "Assay could not take this payment on its payment channel. Ask again to pay. Nothing was charged."
- **Channel-debt renew.** When Assay refuses a paid call with `PAYMENT_DEBT_OUTSTANDING` and `state.json` holds a channel lane, the proxy renews the channel debts this computer signed (never with `--no-paid`; the call is not sent again): `GET /escrow/debts`, then for each `CHANNEL` debt its `(cumulative, calls)` pair re-signed with fresh times on the lane the debt names (`channel.channelId`) and sent to `POST /escrow/debts/{intentId}/voucher` (the SDK's `renewChannelDebts` steps). Only when that lane is this computer's and the pair was served there (or got no answer): never a larger pair, a pair the server refused, another lane, or a debt naming no lane. At most one signature per lane per run and one renewing run a minute (within it the debts are only listed). Not added again to the spend record. The agent then reads: "Assay is collecting an earlier payment your agent already signed. Paid calls usually resume within about 5 minutes. This call was not charged." when every debt is being collected; "Your agent no longer has the payment record for an earlier channel payment. Settle it in the Assay app at {WEB}/funding once it is offered there." for a channel debt it cannot renew (`state.json` deleted, another computer or profile); "Assay could not collect an earlier payment your agent signed yet. Call again in a minute or two. This call was not charged." when only channel debts are left that could not be renewed just now; otherwise (a per-call debt) the usual "Settle it at {WEB}/funding". A proxy with no lane never calls `/escrow/debts`. `PAYMENT_DEBT_OUTSTANDING` on a channel voucher re-arms the lane. When one of the debts being collected is a per-call debt a person settled in the portal (a per-call `REPLACING` debt, also a channel debt moved to a per-call replacement), the agent reads the neutral "Assay is collecting an earlier payment. Paid calls usually resume within about 5 minutes. This call was not charged." instead.
  - A `CHANNEL` debt of another wallet of the same organization (its `payer` is not the list's `account`; the paid-call refusal is organization-wide) is never signed (`other_wallet`), and the agent reads "Another wallet in your organization owes an earlier payment. Paid calls resume once it is settled by that wallet's agent or in the Assay app. This call was not charged." A renew refused for good (`CHANNEL_DESYNC`, `CHANNEL_NOT_OFFERED`, `CHANNEL_SIGNER_EXPIRING`, voucher binding/signature/malformed/delegate codes) gives the portal text instead of "Call again in a minute or two".
- `PAYMENT_UNSETTLED_LIMIT`, on the unsigned call or after a voucher, reads "Waiting for earlier payments to settle on chain; retry in about N minutes. Nothing was charged." (N from `retryAfter`, rounded up; "1 minute" for one). The lane is re-armed.
- The chain reader reads `channelBooks(key, to)`, and the guards' chain state carries the payment key's window cap.
